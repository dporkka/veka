Veka first-sight vocabulary pilot

Write what unfamiliar words might mean. There are no answer hints. This is research, not a language proficiency test.

Before starting

Everything runs locally in the browser. Nothing is sent to a server, persisted, or scored. You may quit without submitting. To save results, download the CSV at the end; refreshing beforehand loses answers.

Use only the participant session JSON supplied by the researcher, never the separate answer key.

What might this word mean?

One attempt per word. Please do not use a dictionary. Browser timings are approximate, not calibrated reaction times.
