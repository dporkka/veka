# Veka 0.3 — known limits

This is an engineered reference grammar and computer-tool prototype, not a speaking community or proof of superior information efficiency.

- No real speech recordings, listener or speaker participants, error observations, measured duration or training studies. The score tool consumes **only provided human measurements**.
- The 2,262 one-feature near pairs in the automatic audit are **not** 2,262 acoustically confusable pairs; orthographic/abstract phonological proximity is an imperfect proxy. The ten digit codes need actual acoustic testing.
- A 932-syllable theoretical inventory does **not** correspond to measured coding entropy, lexical learning capacity or spoken throughput.
- Four tonal words still exist in canonical lexicon. Toneless alternatives are separate experimental vocabulary variants and may introduce new confusability.
- Quantifiers do not have variable-binding across multiple noun phrases. Number-unit syntax labels units but does not implement dimensional analysis, fractions, scientific notation or arithmetic operators.
- Logical implication is material, not the everyday causal use of “if.” Questions and commands are not Boolean propositions and are disallowed in truth-functional composition.
- Existing 24 compression stimuli disproportionately favor compressibility and only verify syntactic equivalence under idealized discourse candidates. The 8 additional controls are a small start, not a representative corpus.
- Conservative reference recovery still assumes a supplied, mutually established context. This software cannot determine what a human listener actually understood.
- The script has no font, IME, Unicode code points, or audio screen-reader implementation; romanization is the accessible reference.
- No study randomization software or CSV scorer substitutes for experimental preregistration, controlled acoustic recording, informed consent and statistical uncertainty analysis.

## Next research priorities

1. Audio pilot (human recordings and volunteers) with the 7 ABX target pairs, tone/no-tone comparisons and diverse language backgrounds.
2. Freeze or redesign ambiguous high-frequency forms from actual error matrices before teaching large cohorts; keep backward compatibility / versioning deliberate.
3. Increase realistic ambiguous, noncompressible, lengthy, repair-driven discourse examples and real conversational corpora.
4. Implement explicit first-order variable binding, scope, count semantics and SI dimensional syntax with formal proof tests.
5. Validate the Veka writing system for rapid reading and typing independently of the spoken-language claims.

## Veka 0.5 additional limitations

- All six proposed suffix spellings are candidates only, not canonical lexical entries and not human-tested. Legal phonotactics and orthographic distances are weak proxies for acoustic distinguishability.
- The correlative matrix is a typed semantic table, **not** a complete spoken/written grammar: only four existing quantified noun phrases have surface realizations.
- Derivational type contracts do not establish that there is a real-world instrument, agent, patient, or place for every verb. Only direct verb-root derivation is permitted; nested derivations are deliberately rejected.
- The optional parser accepts derived word classes and unchanged valency only. It does not implement compositional event semantics, multi-quantifier scope, modality or relative clauses.
