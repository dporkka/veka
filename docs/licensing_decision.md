# Veka distribution licensing decision — pending

**No license is selected or granted by this document.** Public GitHub visibility is not a license. This decision requires the authorization of the copyright holder and potentially contributors.

## Recommended options to evaluate

- Author-owned **source code**: consider MIT or Apache-2.0. Apache-2.0 has explicit patent terms; MIT is shorter.
- Author-owned **educational text and lesson materials**: consider Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0) for attribution, or CC0 for broad unrestricted reuse.
- **Third-party linguistic publications, images, dictionary entries, recordings and contributions**: confirm provenance and permission individually. Veka's license cannot grant rights in someone else's work.
- Decide on community contributions, attribution, participant consent, recordings, and private study data handling.

## Before a public launch

Verify rights, explicitly approve license choices, add official LICENSE and content license files in a separate PR, update repository contribution terms and learner pages. Treat reuse and broad distribution as legally uncertain until that decision is made.
