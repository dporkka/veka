# Veka 0.7 — six-language comprehension cohort (research-only)

**Status:** reproducible pilot design; **not an improvement proven by human
subjects**. No dictionary, reference grammar, syntactic parser, spelling
rules, or tonal inventory is modified.

## Which languages and why

The initial study strata are English (`en`), Mandarin Chinese (`cmn`),
Spanish (`es`), Hindi (`hi`), Portuguese (`pt`), and Indonesian (`id`).
These are **six recruitment languages**, not a claim that six samples capture
worldwide variation. English, Spanish, Portuguese and Hindi share the
Indo-European family, Mandarin belongs to Sino-Tibetan, and Indonesian to
Austronesian. Do not count the six as six genealogically independent data points.
Other large populations (including Arabic, Bengali and many African language
families) are outside this initial design.

The goal is to test the premise that international-looking vocabulary helps
first-sight **recognition and subsequent learning**, rather than assume
phonetic resemblance to English/Spanish/Portuguese is universal. Individual
second-language proficiency, prior familiarity, education, literacy, exposure
to the Latin script, code-switching and dialect must be separately recorded
under an approved protocol. **A language code is a study stratum, not a
diagnosis of native-language identity or proficiency.**

## Current scope and expected artifact

The input is the *already existing*
`data/international_candidates_v04.csv`: 100 concepts, only **eight** with
phonotactically screened **unverified draft proposals**. Each participant
sees one form per meaning, not both; per participant and per language/concept,
half the presentations use canonical Veka and half the unadopted proposal.
No participant sheet includes the intended meaning or condition. The research
key is separate. There are **zero measured observations** in the repository.

```sh
python3 -m veka international-audit
python3 -m veka international-cohort --participants-per-language 2 \
  --seed 20261101 \
  --participant-csv /secure/participant_trials.csv \
  --answer-key /secure/researcher_key.csv

# Human workflow: independently assess handwritten answers against a
# pre-registered meaning rubric; do not auto-match words to English glosses.
# Create an annotated CSV with:
# trial_id,correct,response_ms,repair_count
# (correct = adjudicated 0/1; repair_count = explicit 0 or above).
python3 -m veka international-cohort-score \
  --answer-key /secure/researcher_key.csv \
  --annotated /secure/annotations.csv
```

`--participants-per-language` must be an even integer 2..200; each pair
of synthetic IDs `V07-<code>-NNN-A/B` receives opposite versions of each
concept. With 2 per language, generation produces 12 separate **pseudonymous
IDs** and 96 written trials. Assign IDs **at random within each recruitment
stratum**, not sequentially to arriving participants. The pilot does not
implement recruitment, allocation concealment, study consent, screening,
translation, a presentation interface, audio, or adjudication.

The scorer rejects missing, duplicated, or unjudged observations. It reports
descriptive accuracy, timings and repairs by **language and condition**, plus
aggregate summaries. These are not causal estimates, do not supply sampling
uncertainty, and are not reliable population rankings. An ordinary user-supplied
CSV cannot establish whether any trial was genuinely conducted.

## Research controls before a real learner study

1. **Localize instructions, not canonical Veka.** Commission independent
   human translation and back-translation of task prompts in all six languages;
   include exact sense definitions and response options. No translations or
   donor etymologies are silently generated by this tool.
2. **Pre-register endpoints.** Primary: blind adjudicated correctness of
   first-sight meaning. Secondary: response latency, repairs, delayed retention
   and learning effort. Analyze participant/item effects, missing responses,
   confidence intervals and multiple comparisons; include an oral/noise arm
   only after reliable human recordings exist.
3. **Randomize and blind.** Allocate A/B IDs independently from enrollment
   order; balance participant exposure and item order; prevent participants
   and judges from seeing the researcher key. Publish a study design and an
   exclusion policy before collecting responses.
4. **Record confounders.** Recruitment language is not necessarily L1.
   Record proficiency, literacy, known loanwords, prior Veka exposure,
   keyboard accessibility and exact presentation modality; obtain consent
   and protect identifiable responses outside git.
5. **Set safety/compatibility gates.** A candidate should not become
   canonical without independently validated donor provenance, evidence
   of semantic equivalence, cross-language learning benefit, separately
   measured spoken intelligibility, and a versioned migration plan.

## Interpretation limits

Even perfect results in this pilot would speak only to **eight handpicked
written stimuli**, not the complete 1,025-entry Veka lexicon or a six-language
universal grammar. A balanced synthetic fixture proves scheduling logic,
not understanding. Short canonical roots, regular word formation and
unambiguous reference grammar remain the default unless evidence favors a
specific, reviewed change. Longer internationally recognizable words may
increase speaking time and acoustic confusion. Do not call this a language
improvement until real, blinded learner outcomes justify it.
